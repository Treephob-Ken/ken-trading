## 🏎️ Step 1: Matching Strategy to Market Regime

No single trading strategy makes money in all market conditions. A trend-following strategy will lose money in a sideways range, and a range-bound grid bot will blow up in a strong trend.

Scroll down to the **Market Regime Model** at the bottom of the page to analyze the active market condition:
*   **Bull Regime (Green Banner)**: The market is in an uptrend. Use trend-following strategies (e.g., **EMA Crossover**, **MACD**, **Supertrend**).
*   **Bear Regime (Red Banner)**: The market is in a downtrend. Look for short signals or stay in cash.
*   **Sideways Regime (Orange Banner)**: The market is flat and range-bound. Perfect for **Grid Trading** and oscillators (e.g., **RSI Reversal**, **Bollinger Bands**, **Stochastic**).

> [!TIP]
> Check the **Persistence Score** (the probability that the current regime continues) and **Conviction Score** (the mathematical difference between Bullish and Bearish probabilities). High conviction means a strong, stable regime.

## 📊 Step 2: How to Run a Backtest

1.  **Select Your Market Pair**: Use the **Market Pair** search bar in the sidebar. You can search any Binance trading pair (e.g., `BTC/USDT`, `ETH/USDT`, `SOL/USDT`).
2.  **Choose Your Timeframe**:
    *   **15m / 30m**: Best for active day-trading or scalping.
    *   **1h / 4h**: Best for swing trading (trades last days).
    *   **1d**: Best for macro position trading (trades last weeks/months).
3.  **Choose Your Strategy**: Select an indicator from the **Strategy** dropdown list.
4.  **Adjust Parameters**: Drag the sliders in the sidebar. The backtest will run instantly in the background, updating the chart and results card.

## 📈 Step 3: Reading the Performance Metrics

When the backtest completes, analyze the **Results** card. Look for these critical metrics:

| Metric | What It Means | Target Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Strategy Return** | The total profit/loss generated by the strategy. | Higher than Buy & Hold |
| **Buy & Hold** | The return if you just bought the asset and did nothing. | Benchmark comparison |
| **Max Drawdown** | The largest peak-to-trough drop in your account balance. | Lower than 15% (for sanity) |
| **Profit Factor** | Gross profits divided by gross losses. | **> 1.2** is good, **> 1.8** is elite |
| **Sharpe Ratio** | Reward per unit of total volatility/risk. | **> 1.0** is good, **> 2.0** is outstanding |
| **Sortino Ratio** | Reward per unit of downside risk only. | **> 1.5** is good, **> 2.5** is outstanding |
| **Expectancy** | The average return percentage you expect per trade. | Positive (**> 0.0%**) |

> [!WARNING]
> If your **Strategy Return** is lower than **Buy & Hold** in a strong uptrend, you are taking active trading risk for less reward than holding. Re-optimize or choose a different indicator.

## 🛡️ Step 4: Position Sizing and Safety Exits

Risk management is the only thing that keeps a trader from going bankrupt. Use the **Risk Settings** card:

### 1. Position Sizing Modes
*   **Fixed Size**: Trades the exact same dollar amount every time (e.g., $1,000 per trade).
*   **Compounding**: Trades a percentage of your growing account balance. Maximizes returns during winning streaks, but increases drawdown during losing streaks.
*   **Volatility Sizing (ATR)**: Calculates how much the price is moving (using Average True Range). If the market is highly volatile, it shrinks your trade size and widens your stop-loss. If the market is calm, it increases your trade size and tightens your stop-loss. This keeps your dollar risk constant!

### 2. Stop Loss & Take Profit
*   **Stop Loss (SL)**: Your emergency exit. If a trade drops by this percentage, it cuts your losses immediately. **Never trade without a Stop Loss.**
*   **Take Profit (TP)**: Your target exit. Closes the trade to lock in gains once the target is hit.

## 🔬 Step 5: Advanced Integrity Checks

Before you deploy any settings live, scroll to the bottom and verify:

### 1. Walk-Forward Validation (The Overfitting Filter)
*   **Overfitting** happens when you tweak strategy settings so perfectly to past data that they memorize noise, then fail in live markets.
*   The Walk-Forward engine trains parameters on historical segments and tests them on unseen forward segments.
*   **Verdict**: Look for **Robust**. If the status is **Fragile** or **Overfit**, change your timeframe or simplify your settings.

### 2. Strategy Correlation Matrix
*   If you trade multiple strategies, you want them to be uncorrelated (different trades at different times).
*   **Blue/Green Cells**: Low correlation (< 0.20). Excellent for trading together.
*   **Red Cells**: High correlation (> 0.60). Avoid running both—they will double your risk on the same trades.

## ⚙️ Step 6: Optimizing and Running a Grid Bot (Sideways Markets)

Grid trading places a grid of buy and sell limit orders around the current price. It stacks small profits as price moves up and down.

### 1. The Rules of Grid Optimization
*   Use the **Grid Optimizer** tab on the website.
*   Choose between **Arithmetic Grid** (equal dollar gaps) and **Geometric Grid** (equal percentage gaps). **Geometric is highly recommended for crypto.**
*   **Spacing ÷ Breakeven**: This number **must be at least 3.0×**. If it is lower, your grid levels are too close together, and trading fees will eat up all your profits!

### 2. Live Bot Deployment (Step-by-Step)
1.  **Open the Local Dashboard**: Run the following in your terminal:
    ```powershell
    cd c:\Users\ken25\trading\bot
    npm.cmd start
    ```
    Then open `http://localhost:3001` in your browser.
2.  **Export the Config**: On the website's Grid Optimizer page, enter your **Investment Budget** (e.g., `500` USDC) and **Leverage** (e.g., `1`x or `2`x). Click **Download grid.config.json**.
3.  **Deploy**: Drag and drop the downloaded JSON file directly into the local dashboard. Click **Start Bot**.
4.  **Monitor**: The bot will automatically submit stop-loss and take-profit orders directly to the exchange (Hyperliquid). You can track filled trades, real-time equity curves, and active logs directly on the dashboard.

## 💡 Quick Cheat Sheet for Success

1.  **Regime Check first**: Don't run a grid bot in a trending market. Use the Markov Regime panel.
2.  **Paper Trade first**: Always run new configurations on the **Hyperliquid Testnet** for at least 24 hours before moving to Mainnet.
3.  **Watch the Liquidation Price**: If using leverage (e.g., > 3x), keep your Stop Loss well *above* the liquidation price. The dashboard safety panel will warn you if your Stop Loss is unreachable.
